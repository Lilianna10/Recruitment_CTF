CyberQuest: Cybersecurity Club Capture the Flag

Rules

  • Pick any challenge, in any order. Find the flag to solve it! You can submit flags using the Google Form.
  • Flags are formatted as: CTF{answer}
  • Note: the CTF and curly braces are part of the answer.
  • Answers are case-insensitive, I will review flags for correct answers and hand out rewards during the first club meeting.
  • AI is allowed, but please do not copy and paste anything directly into it.
  • Please do not share answers so others can enjoy the CTF.
  • Please do not spam random guesses into the Google forms.
  • Lastly, have fun! If you need any hints or help you can message me on Teams.
